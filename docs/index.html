Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Download LatteLink from the App Store, then sign up with your email address. You'll need to provide your college information, location, and favorite coffee preferences to complete your profile.
Is LatteLink only for college students?
Yes! LatteLink is designed exclusively for college students to help build a safe, authentic community of coffee lovers on campus.
How does matching work?
Browse profiles of students at your college and nearby. Swipe right if you're interested, left if not. When two people swipe right on each other, it's a match! You can then start chatting and plan your coffee date.
What are premium features?
Premium features include virtual gifts (coffee cups, hearts, roses, cakes, stars, and sparkles), icebreaker questions to start conversations, priority messages to stand out, and read receipts to see when your message was read.
How do I unmatch with someone?
Go to your Matches tab, find the match you want to remove, swipe left on their conversation, and tap "Unmatch". This will remove them from your matches and prevent further messaging.
Can I change my profile information?
Yes! Go to your Profile tab and tap "Edit Profile" to update your photos, bio, coffee preferences, or any other information.
How do I add or change photos?
In your Profile tab, tap on your profile photo to add or change photos. You can upload photos from your camera roll or take a new one.
What are icebreaker questions?
Icebreaker questions are fun, coffee-themed conversation starters that help you get to know your matches better. They appear as suggestions when you're messaging someone new.
How does the café map work?
The Map tab shows local coffee shops near your location. You can see ratings, get directions, and discover new spots recommended by other LatteLink users.
Is my data private and secure?
Yes! We take your privacy seriously. Your data is never shared with third parties, and you have full control over your profile visibility. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings tab.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your profile, matches, and messages. This action cannot be undone.
I'm having technical issues. What should I do?
Try restarting the app first. If the problem persists, please contact our support team with details about the issue you're experiencing.

Safety Tips

  • Always meet in public places for your first coffee date
  • Tell a friend or roommate where you're going and who you're meeting
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels off, it probably is
  • Never share personal information like your address or financial details
  • Report any suspicious or inappropriate behavior immediately
  • Keep conversations on the app until you feel comfortable

Getting Started Guide

  1. Complete Your Profile: Add photos, write a bio, and share your coffee preferences
  2. Set Your Location: Enable location services to find matches nearby
  3. Start Swiping: Browse profiles and swipe right on people you're interested in
  4. Match & Chat: When you match, start a conversation with a message or icebreaker
  5. Plan Your Date: Use the map to find a great café and meet up!

Community Guidelines

LatteLink is a respectful community. We expect all users to:

  • Be authentic and honest in your profile
  • Treat others with respect and kindness
  • Use appropriate language and behavior
  • Not spam, harass, or abuse other users
  • Report any violations of these guidelines
